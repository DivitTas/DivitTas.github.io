I love Godot. I also needed multiplayer to just work. The unsentimental version of that decision.

## Why Godot first

I started in Godot 4 because I know it well and the iteration speed is excellent for prototyping. The first two months of Catastrophe Cafe were built there: basic cat movement, a simple kitchen layout, the core interaction model.

## Why we switched

Multiplayer. Godot's high-level networking is good, but it requires more manual work than I had bandwidth for while also designing the game. Unity's Netcode for GameObjects — imperfect as it is — has more solved problems and more documentation for the specific patterns I needed (object ownership, state synchronization, latency compensation).

The switch took two weeks. Most systems ported cleanly. The cat personalities required a full rewrite because of how Godot's signals differ from Unity's event system.

## What I miss

Gdscript. Just being honest.
