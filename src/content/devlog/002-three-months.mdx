A short year-end checkpoint. Things I expected. Things I didn't.

## What I expected

That building a co-op game would be harder than a single-player game. (Correct.)

That cat AI would be a solved problem I could implement quickly. (Incorrect.)

## What I didn't expect

That the kitchen layout would matter this much. The spacing between stations determines whether co-op feels like teamwork or chaos. Too close: players collide constantly. Too far: they might as well be playing alone. We've rebuilt the floor plan four times.

Also: that people would get attached to specific cats after ten minutes with a prototype. Biscuit already has fans.

## Where we are

Core loop works. Networking is held together with optimism and latency compensation. The cats have personalities. The coffee spills correctly. This is a real game now, not just a jam project that kept going.
