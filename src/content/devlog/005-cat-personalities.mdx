Designing cats as small state machines. Plus, why one of them refuses to drink from a bowl.

## The architecture

Each cat is a small state machine with four base states: Wandering, Curious, Demanding, and Napping. State transitions are driven by a combination of environment (loud noise? fire?) and personality weights.

Personality is three floats: curiosity, grumpiness, and hunger urgency. These don't change what the states *do* — they change how quickly the cat moves between them and how it behaves within each state.

## The cat that won't drink from bowls

Cat #17 — internally called "Biscuit" — has a curiosity weight of 0.9 and a grumpiness weight of 0.8. In testing, these values interacted in a way we didn't anticipate: Biscuit would approach a water bowl, sniff it (Curious), decide it was unacceptable (Grumpy), and walk away. Every time.

We kept it. Some bugs are features.
