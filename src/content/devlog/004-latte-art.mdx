You can make a passable rosetta with eight particles and a curve. Here is the curve.

## The approach

Latte art is a minigame. You have a limited window — the pour — to trace a pattern. We represent the pour as a stream of particles that fall into the cup and interact with a surface-tension simulation (very simple: particles push each other laterally and settle toward the center).

A rosetta pattern requires eight particles placed along a bezier curve, with specific timing between each. The result isn't photorealistic, but it reads as latte art to anyone who's ever made one.

## Why it works

The secret is the pour angle. Tilting the cup slightly (represented in-game by a held button) changes the flow direction. Players learn this intuitively because it mirrors real espresso technique. The mechanic teaches itself.
