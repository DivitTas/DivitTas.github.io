The pitch document, lightly cleaned up. Cats. Friends. Mild incompetence.

## The pitch

Four friends. A cafe. Thirty cats. The health inspector comes on Tuesday.

That was the original pitch document. It fit in a text message. The version I sent to my friends was: "chaotic co-op kitchen game but with cats instead of customers and the cats are also the problem."

## Why co-op

Because the best gaming memories I have are shared ones. Because Overcooked exists and people love it. Because I wanted to make something my friends would actually play.

## Why cats

Cats are chaos engines with legible motivations. You always know *why* the cat knocked the cup off the counter. (Spite. It was spite.) That legibility makes them funny rather than frustrating — and funny chaos is the core tone I'm after.

## What this is actually about

A game about helping each other with a problem that keeps getting worse. The cats aren't the antagonists. The cats are the condition you're operating under.
