So I'm making a game. It's called Catastrophe Cafe.

The concept is pretty simple: you and some friends are running a cat cafe. Serve customers, manage the cafe, try to keep things from falling apart. It sounds manageable until you get a few orange cats on your hands, and then suddenly it really isn't.

It's still very much a work in progress, but I have a lot of ideas I'm genuinely excited about and I want somewhere to talk about them as I build. That's what this devlog is for.

The multiplayer angle came from a pretty honest place: my friends and I are running out of games to play together. So I figured I'd just make one. It also helps that I love cats. Dogs too, but I've never seen a dog cafe, so here we are.

I'll be posting updates here as things move forward. No schedule, just whenever there's something worth writing about.
