Three rewrites in, the cats finally walk around the espresso machine instead of through it. Here's the navmesh trick I should have used in March.

## What I tried

The first three approaches all had the same flaw: I was treating the cats like player characters and giving them a full navigation agent. Cats don't navigate. Cats *wander with intention*. The espresso machine wasn't an obstacle to route around — it was a surface to consider jumping onto.

The fix was splitting movement into two systems: a coarse pathfinding layer (A* on a baked navmesh) and a local steering layer (simple avoidance radius). The coarse layer finds a general path. The local layer handles the last half-meter of "I can't quite fit through here."

## What worked

Baking the navmesh with a smaller agent radius than the cat's visual size. The cats look bigger than they navigate. This gives them a slightly floating quality — which, honestly, is accurate to real cats.
